# Private data

The Jiangzhou woodblock New Year print dataset used in the study is not publicly distributed due to cultural-heritage and authorization considerations. Data access may be requested from the corresponding author and is subject to the relevant permissions.

Place authorised, standardised patches in train, val, and test subdirectories under a private data root. PNG, JPEG, and TIFF files are supported. Split source images before patch extraction; never place patches from one source image in multiple subsets.

Dataset-specific ICC profiles, white-balance matrices, source-image identifiers, and acquisition metadata are not included.
